Finance OS

A personal finance tool. Privacy note.

Finance OS is a personal tool with one user: its owner, Nasser Mohamed. It is not offered to anyone else.

With his permission, it reads his own Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Calendar data, sends him one summary e-mail a week, and keeps a spreadsheet it creates in his Drive up to date.

Everything it reads stays on his own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and he can withdraw access at any time from his Google Account settings.
